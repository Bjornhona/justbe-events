### What data we process

Only what the user voluntarily provides through the contact form:

- Name and surname
- Company
- Email address
- Telephone (optional)
- Event type, approximate date and estimated number of attendees
- The content of the message

For technical reasons only, the server also logs the IP address and basic connection data, necessary for the security and correct operation of the site.

No special categories of data are collected. Please do not include sensitive information in the message field.

### Why we process it

- To respond to the enquiry received.
- To prepare and send a service proposal where appropriate.
- To maintain the communication arising from that enquiry.

Data is not used for unsolicited marketing and is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

### Legal basis

- **Consent of the data subject** (art. 6.1.a GDPR), given by ticking the acceptance box on the form.
- **Pre-contractual measures** taken at the data subject's request (art. 6.1.b GDPR), where the enquiry concerns the contracting of services.

### How long we keep it

Data is retained for as long as the relationship or interest in the service continues and thereafter for the periods legally required for the limitation of liability. Once those periods have elapsed, the data is deleted.

Where an enquiry does not lead to a commercial relationship, the data is deleted within a maximum of one year from the last contact.

### Recipients and processors

Data is not disclosed to third parties except where legally required. The following providers act as processors under an article 28 GDPR agreement:

## 3. Cookie policy

### Cookies used on this site

This website currently uses **no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies**.

Only strictly necessary cookies and technologies are used, such as those that remember the selected language. These are exempt from the consent requirement under article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE, as they are essential to providing the service requested by the user.

### Managing cookies

Users may configure their browser to block or delete cookies. As only technical cookies are used, blocking them may affect the correct operation of some features, such as language selection.

### Changes to this policy

If tools that install analytics or third-party cookies are added in future, this policy will be updated and prior consent will be requested through a consent management system.
